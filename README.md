## Building LingualGain

LingualGain is a language-learning app built around short, interactive conversations. Learners can listen to dialogues, explore unfamiliar expressions, practise responding and pick up where they left off.

I want learning to feel like a conversation rather than a collection of disconnected exercises. That means keeping explanations close to where they're needed, making practice interactive and helping learners build on what they've already learned.

The app is still in development, and I'm keeping the source code private for now. You can explore the features and some of the engineering behind them in the [**LingualGain showcase**](https://github.com/vincentunruh/lingualgain-showcase).

## Product & Business Thinking

LingualGain started with a simple question. How can I help someone move from recognising a phrase to actually using it in a conversation?

That question shapes a lot of the decisions I make. Explanations should help without interrupting the conversation, typing should remain an option when speaking isn't available, and learners should have opportunities to practise things they've seen before. Even behind the scenes, I'd rather spend more time checking learning content than risk showing someone an explanation that doesn't fit.

But building features is only part of it. I also want to understand whether learners can use what they've practised without help, remember it later and actually want to come back.

And then there's the business side. Who would benefit most from LingualGain? How do I reach those people? What would make the app valuable enough for them to pay for?

Those are the questions I want to answer as I keep building, so that real user needs shape the product rather than just adding more features.

## Selected Engineering Work

- **Navigation and recording.** Handling cancelled recordings, microphone cleanup and responses that arrive after someone has already moved to another screen.
- **Contextual explanations.** Making sure word explanations reflect the actual conversation rather than just an isolated dictionary meaning.
- **Content consistency.** Keeping dialogues, explanations and exercises in sync as learning content changes.
